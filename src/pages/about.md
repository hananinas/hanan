I'm Hanan Choudhary Hadayat. I study Computer Science and Engineering at DTU and spend much of my time building software that I wish existed.

By day I'm a part-time software engineer at Context& and teach Python and machine learning as a TA at Copenhagen Business School. I founded Unravel AI, and I am writing my MSc thesis on program analysis as scalable oversight for coding agents: making long-running agents something you can check instead of trust.

I work on [Elusive](https://elusivecph.com), a way to search for clothes across stores, and [Unravel Search](https://search.uai.dk), a search tool for AI agents. Most of my other building time goes into agent tooling: Arvel (an agent workspace, formerly Khadim) and Blop (a platform for observing and testing coding agents). The common thread is making complex tools easier to use without hiding how they work.

Outside software I run Elusive as a slow-fashion project, and I enter Kaggle competitions. A Gemma 4 agent-build competition is open right now, with an entry deadline of November 25.

I write about projects as I build them. Older posts stay here so you can see the path, not just the current version.
