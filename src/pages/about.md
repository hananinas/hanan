I'm Hanan Choudhary Hadayat. I study Computer Science and Engineering at DTU and spend much of my time building software that I wish existed.

By day I'm a part-time software engineer at Context& and teach Python and machine learning as a TA at Copenhagen Business School. I founded Unravel AI, an AI software house in Copenhagen. My MSc thesis, with DTU Chemical Engineering and LUT University, is on LLM agents operating a real-time chemical reactor: the agent reads sensors and moves the plant's inputs while a safety interlock and a logging harness stay outside it.

I work on [Elusive](https://elusivecph.com), a way to search for clothes across stores, and on [Unravel](https://uai.dk), where we build custom agent systems for companies. Most of my other building time goes into agent tooling: Arvel (an agent workspace, formerly Khadim) and Blop (a platform for observing and testing coding agents). The common thread is making complex tools easier to use without hiding how they work.

Outside software I run Elusive as a slow-fashion project, and I enter Kaggle competitions. A Gemma 4 agent-build competition is open right now, with an entry deadline of November 25.

I write about projects as I build them. Older posts stay here so you can see the path, not just the current version.
